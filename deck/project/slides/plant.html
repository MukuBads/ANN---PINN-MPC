The plant: a non-isothermal tubular reactor

Axial dispersion + Arrhenius kinetics (Pe = 10, Da = 0.8, E = 15). Input: jacket temperature u ∈ [0.95, 1.15]. Output: outlet concentration. The static gain changes about eightfold across the range.

Review 2 · Causality-aware PINN-MPC · 3

Two coupled PDEs, concentration and temperature, with Danckwerts boundary conditions. The “real plant” in all experiments is a 101-node finite-difference model; it is also the perfect-model reference controller. It never produces training labels. The eightfold gain change is why a linear model would not be enough.
