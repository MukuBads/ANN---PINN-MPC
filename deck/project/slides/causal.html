The one change under test: how time slices are weighted

Uniform

L = mean over slices of Lᵢ

Every time slice counts equally from the first iteration. The optimiser can fit late times before early times are right.

Causal

wᵢ = exp(−ε · Σ Lⱼ over j < i)

Slice i only switches on once earlier slices are solved. Our change: weights are computed per initial condition, not per batch.

Same architecture, sampler, schedule and budget (8,000 Adam + 600 L-BFGS steps). Only the weighting differs.

Review 2 · Causality-aware PINN-MPC · 5

Causal weighting comes from Wang, Sankaran and Perdikaris, 2024. The weight on time slice i decays with the summed residual of all earlier slices, so the network has to get early times right first. The paper defines it for one trajectory; a control batch has many initial conditions and inputs, so we compute the weights per instance.
