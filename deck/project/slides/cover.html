Causality-aware PINN-MPC for a tubular reactor

A physics-informed neural network as the prediction model inside MPC, trained with uniform or causal temporal weighting

Mukund Dinesh Badsewal (110123068) · Revant Sinha (110123088)

Instrumentation & Control Engineering · NIT Tiruchirappalli · 16 October 2026

Introduce the project in one line: we replace the PDE solve inside MPC with a physics-informed neural network, and we test whether causal training makes that network a better controller model. Review 1 was the proposal; today we show the working model, the results, and what went wrong along the way.
