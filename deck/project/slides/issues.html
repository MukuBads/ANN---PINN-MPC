02 · Performance issues

Where the model still falls short

Causal weighting gives no gain yet

Across 3 seeds, causal and uniform tie (IAE 0.322 vs 0.326 without bias correction). The expected improvement has not appeared.

ε annealing never triggers

Causal weights peak at 0.94, under the 0.99 threshold, so ε stays at 1: causal training only runs at its weakest setting.

Training stops before convergence

The PDE residual is still falling at the end of the budget; the causal arm ends higher (4.4 vs 3.1 × 10⁻³).

Gap to the perfect model

IAE is 5–7 % above FD-MPC without bias correction, and uniform’s 8-hop error varies about ±40 % between seeds.

Review 2 · Causality-aware PINN-MPC · 4

The model works, but not yet as well as it should. Causal weighting was the idea under test and it has not shown a benefit. The most likely reason is the training configuration: ε never leaves its starting value and training ends while the residual is still falling. These point directly at the hyperparameters, which is what we are tuning now.
