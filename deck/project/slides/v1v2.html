v1 training profiles broke the inlet boundary condition. Fixing that made both networks 3–6× more accurate and removed causal’s v1 disadvantage.

Review 2 · Causality-aware PINN-MPC · 11 · FD-MPC reference IAE: 0.306

In v1, the sampled training profiles violated the Danckwerts inlet condition, with a median mismatch of 0.05. That left a large residual at the inlet at time zero, so the first time slice never got solved, and causal weighting kept every later slice dimmed. The v2 encoder imposes both boundary conditions exactly, through a constrained least-squares fit.
